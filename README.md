# NexaAI – AI Product & Customer Intelligence

## Project Overview

NexaAI is an end-to-end data analytics portfolio project based on a fictional AI SaaS business.

The project focuses on analysing customer behaviour, subscription performance, AI product usage, payment transactions and customer satisfaction to generate meaningful business insights.

The objective is to transform raw data into actionable insights using SQL, MySQL and Power BI.

## Business Objectives

- Analyse customer acquisition and growth trends.
- Understand subscription distribution and cancellation patterns.
- Evaluate AI model usage, response latency and failure rates.
- Analyse payment performance and transaction outcomes.
- Identify customer satisfaction trends and retention patterns.
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard to support business decisions.

## Tools & Technologies

- MySQL
- SQL
- Microsoft Power BI
- DAX
- Excel

## Database Structure

The project contains five relational tables:

1. Customers – Customer information, signup dates, country and acquisition channel.
2. Subscriptions – Subscription plans, pricing and subscription status.
3. AI Usage Logs – AI model requests, tokens, latency, request status and estimated cost.
4. Payments – Payment amounts and transaction statuses.
5. Customer Feedback – Customer ratings and feedback categories.

## Key Business Insights

- 500 customers analysed across the dataset.
- 350 active subscriptions, representing 70% of subscriptions.
- 24,000 AI requests analysed across three AI models.
- Overall AI request failure rate of 5%.
- Average customer satisfaction score of 3.00 out of 5.
- Payment success rate of 90%.
- 90-day subscription retention rate of 81.53% based on the project's defined cohort methodology.
